# Data acquisition and verification policy

1. **Do not scrape or bulk-copy proprietary competitor paint databases.** A public page is not proof of permission to redistribute a dataset.
2. Record the source URL, publisher, document title, retrieval date and reuse-rights assessment for every imported record.
3. Keep all imports in pending status. A reviewer must confirm the exact OEM code, color name, model and applicable year range against a reliable source before publishing.
4. Manufacturer names and OEM identifiers are used for factual reference, not to imply endorsement.
5. Paint codes can refer to variants, multi-stage finishes or different regional formulas. Do not guarantee an exact color match.
6. A VIN does not itself encode the vehicle paint code. Do not offer exact VIN lookup without licensed build-record access.
7. For illustrations, create original diagrams; do not reproduce copyrighted manual illustrations without permission.
8. If sources conflict, mark the record pending and explain the uncertainty.
9. Preserve an audit trail for corrections, verification decisions and withdrawn records.
